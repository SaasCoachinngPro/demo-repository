Physics Final Exam

Q1. What is the speed of light in a vacuum?
A) 3 x 10^8 m/s
B) 3 x 10^5 m/s
C) 3 x 10^10 m/s
D) 3 x 10^6 m/s
Ans: A
Explanation: This is a universal physical constant.

Q2. Which of the following is a scalar quantity?
A) Force
B) Velocity
C) Acceleration
D) Temperature
Answer: D
Explanation: Temperature has magnitude but no direction.

Q3. Calculate the numerical value of 5 factorial.
Answer: 120
